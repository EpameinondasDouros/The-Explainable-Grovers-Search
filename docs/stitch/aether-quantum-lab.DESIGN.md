## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a gallery-grade quantum visualization environment where state-of-the-art computational physics meets haute digital craft. The aesthetic rejects cold, sterile wireframes in favor of an ethereal cosmic atmosphere: deep void depths illuminated by controlled quantum luminescence, laser-precise resonance ribbons, and translucent multi-layered frosted glass. 

The aesthetic blends **Elevated Glassmorphism** and **Atmospheric Sci-Fi Futurism**:
- **Atmospheric Depth:** Deep indigo-to-abyss voids serve as an endless canvas, populated by soft volumetric blooms and ambient particle fields.
- **Luminous Precision:** High-energy state shifts and coherence markers glow in vibrant violet, cyan, and amber, simulating radiant light emissions rather than painted pixels.
- **Sophisticated Curvature:** Hyper-refined pill forms, crystalline glass refraction, and hairline iridescent borders project the prestige of an avant-garde computational observatory.

## Colors

The palette is engineered around an immersive cosmic dark mode where luminous frequencies signal quantum states and telemetry:

- **Primary (`#a855f7` - Celestial Violet):** Represents active superposition, coherence fields, and primary operator actions. It radiates an ethereal violet bloom against dark voids.
- **Secondary (`#00f5d4` - Quantum Cyan):** Denotes quantum entanglement, verified pathways, and hyper-precise real-time metrics.
- **Tertiary (`#f59e0b` - Radiant Amber):** Reserved for thermal fluctuations, phase shifts, decoherence warnings, and dynamic energetic nodes.
- **Neutral Void (`#0a0c16` - Deep Cosmic Abyss):** An ultra-dense indigo-black that anchors the canvas, providing infinite negative space for glass refraction and light diffusion.

### Functional Surface Hierarchy
- **Void Background:** `#06070d` to `#0a0c16` via subtle radial field gradients.
- **Glass Panel Surface:** `rgba(16, 20, 38, 0.65)` layered with backdrop blur.
- **Elevated Glass Surface:** `rgba(26, 32, 60, 0.75)` for modals and active parameter trays.
- **Iridescent Borders:** Hairline gradients flowing from `rgba(168, 85, 247, 0.35)` to `rgba(0, 245, 212, 0.20)` down to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`.
- **Text & Telemetry:** Neutral pure `#f8fafc` for hero metrics, `#cbd5e1` for standard data readouts, and `#64748b` for subtle metadata.

## Typography

The typography architecture balances the geometric futurism of **Sora** with the razor-sharp technical clarity of **Geist**:

- **Display & Headlines (Sora):** Sora delivers structural curvature and futuristic presence. Kerning is tightened slightly on large sizes to produce high-density titles reminiscent of astronomical manifests.
- **Body & Data Readouts (Geist):** Geist brings uncompromised legibility to quantum formulas, multi-qubit matrices, and interactive state arrays. Its clean glyph proportions maintain high scanning speeds under high-contrast lighting.
- **Micro Telemetry Labels:** Monospaced and tracking-boosted configurations of Geist are utilized for Qubit identifiers (e.g., `|ψ⟩`, `Q-084: ACTIVE`), status flags, and physical sensor telemetry.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is structured around an adaptive 12-column fluid grid that treats the UI as an optical cockpit over an interactive quantum field:

- **Desktop (1280px+):** 12-column layout with `margin: 3rem` and `gutter: 1.5rem`. Side docks and Bloch sphere telemetry clusters float with dynamic glass margins over the main canvas.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8-column layout with `margin: 2rem` and `gutter: 1.25rem`. Secondary parameter sidebars collapse into elevated slide-over frosted trays.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with `margin-mobile: 1rem` and `gutter-mobile: 0.75rem`. Complex qubit grids reflow into swipeable quantum state modules.
- **Spacing Rhythm:** Internal card structures adhere strictly to the 4px/8px modular base scale. Expansive outer spacing (`space-xl`) prevents glowing elements from bleeding into neighboring interfaces.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not use drop shadows; instead, it is generated through **luminous translucency, backdrop blurs, and spectral blooms**:

1. **Layer 0 (Cosmic Floor):** Pure void (`#0a0c16`) layered with dynamic CSS/SVG radial gradients (`rgba(168, 85, 247, 0.12)` and `rgba(0, 245, 212, 0.08)`) simulating distant nebular coherence.
2. **Layer 1 (Frosted Glass Panels):** Translucent fill `rgba(16, 20, 38, 0.65)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(20px) saturate(180%)`. Bordered by a 1px iridescent rim (`border: 1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`).
3. **Layer 2 (Elevated Cockpits & Modals):** Translucent fill `rgba(22, 28, 52, 0.85)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(32px)`. Outlined with a dual border effect and an outer atmospheric halo: `box-shadow: 0 0 30px -5px rgba(168, 85, 247, 0.25), inset 0 1px 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.20)`.
4. **Layer 3 (Active Quantum Resonators & Orbs):** Elements that radiate raw energy utilize intense multi-stop drop blooms: `box-shadow: 0 0 16px rgba(0, 245, 212, 0.6), 0 0 32px rgba(0, 245, 212, 0.3)`.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a **Pill-Shaped (Level 3)** silhouette philosophy, imparting a fluid, frictionless, and optical instrument aesthetic:

- **Buttons & Status Indicators:** Full pill geometries (`rounded-full` / `9999px`) provide smooth containment for glowing states and interactive triggers.
- **Cards & Data Modules:** Smooth hyper-radii of `2rem` (`rounded-lg`) ensure soft transitions against dark void backdrops, avoiding abrasive hard corners.
- **Overlays & Primary Canvas Windows:** `3rem` (`rounded-xl`) outer corners evoke curved physical lenses and containment fields.
- **Geometry Logic:** Circular orb nodes and continuous curvature paths dominate data visualizations to reflect the probabilistic wave nature of quantum computing.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Glow Button:** Fully pill-shaped with background gradient `linear-gradient(135deg, #a855f7 0%, #7928ca 100%)`. Inset highlight on the top edge and an ethereal outer bloom (`0 0 24px rgba(168, 85, 247, 0.45)`). Sora font, medium weight.
- **Quantum Cyan Ghost Button:** Transparent pill with an iridescent hairline border (`rgba(0, 245, 212, 0.4)`). Text in `#00f5d4`. On hover, fills with `rgba(0, 245, 212, 0.1)` accompanied by an intensified cyan halo.

### Chips & Badges
- **Pill Telemetry Badges:** Micro-height pill capsules featuring an illuminated pulsing orb (4px radial glow indicator) paired with uppercase mono text. 
- **States:** Violet for *Superposition*, Cyan for *Entangled*, Amber for *Decohering / Fluctuating*.

### Cards & Panels
- **Resonance Glass Cards:** Frosted glass panels (`backdrop-filter: blur(20px)`) with hairline iridescent gradients that change edge illumination based on mouse tracking. Internal padding set to `space-lg`.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Quantum Parameter Inputs:** Dark sunken fields (`rgba(8, 10, 20, 0.8)`) with recessed inner shadows. When focused, transitions to a razor-thin `#a855f7` border with a surrounding `12px` ambient violet aura.
- **Toggle Sliders:** Encased in glass pill tracks with a gleaming luminous orb thumb that leaves a soft particle trail upon sliding.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- **Coherence Selectors:** Circular rings with an internal glowing node. Checking an item animates a blooming pulse wave radiating outward from the central coordinate.

### Specialty Quantum Lab Components
- **Orbital Bloch Sphere Monitors:** Luminous 3D wireframe spheres embedded within circular glass masks, featuring orbital rings rendered in shimmering cyan and purple gradients.
- **Quantum Ribbon Waveforms:** Dynamic SVG/Canvas splines with multi-stop glowing linear gradients reflecting real-time phase probability vectors.
